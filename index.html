FreeWheelers Verified

Access you can actually trust.

Most apps show a wheelchair icon that someone clicked once. We hold every place to a real standard, built from the mobility checklist behind Art Thomas's own journey back to walking, driving, and traveling on his own.

🚪 Getting in

  • Step-free entrance or a real ramp, not "just one small step"
  • Doorways wide enough for every chair (32 inches or more)
  • Accessible parking or a safe drop-off at the door

🧭 Getting around

  • A wheelchair-accessible restroom
  • Clear paths inside, no squeezing between tables
  • Accessible seating and table height

🛏️ Staying over (hotels)

  • A roll-in shower, or grab bars that hold real weight
  • Transfer-friendly bed height

🤝 The human part

  • Staff who help without being asked twice
  • Menu options to match how your group likes to eat

What the badge means today: a green FreeWheelers Approved means live map data confirms an accessible entrance and an accessible restroom. Where we are headed: full 10-point, on-site verification with partner venues, so the badge means someone who lives this checklist checked it in person.
