FreeWheelers matches you with hotels, restaurants, and activities that already work for your group — real access details, ratings, and reviews from live Google Maps data. All that's left to plan is how much fun you're going to have.

🥩 Eat runs on feel-good fuel: fast food, fried, dessert-first, and sugar-sauce BBQ are filtered out. Meat-first spots (steakhouse, grill, seafood) get the badge — food that keeps everyone feeling their best.

🌻 No seed oils, either. We believe your brain and nervous system deserve better fuel. Every restaurant card has a Seed Oil Check button — verify what a kitchen cooks with at Seed Oil Scout before you book the table.

💡 Carnivore hack: almost any meat-first spot works if you order right — at a burger place, get the patties only and skip the bun. ⛔ Hard pass: Chick-fil-A. The ingredient list doesn't meet our feel-good fuel standards — it will never appear in FreeWheelers results.

⛔ Vegetarian restaurants — not recommended. Plant-based menus are loaded with oxalates, compounds linked to kidney stones, and we believe a plant-heavy diet is inflammatory and works against the brain and nervous system (see Dr. Anthony Chaffee's research library at dranthonychaffee.com). They stay in the list so you can see access details — but each one carries a critical Not Recommended warning.

✅ FreeWheelers Verified — access you can actually trust

Other apps show you a wheelchair icon someone clicked once. We hold every place to a real standard — built from the same mobility checklist that took our founder, Art Thomas of LWA Strong, from a wheelchair every neurologist called permanent to walking, driving, and traveling on his own.

🚪 Getting in

  • Step-free entrance or a real ramp — not "just one small step"
  • Doorways wide enough for every chair (32″+)
  • Accessible parking or a safe drop-off at the door

🧭 Getting around

  • Wheelchair-accessible restroom
  • Clear paths inside — no squeezing between tables
  • Accessible seating and table height

🛏️ Staying over (hotels)

  • Roll-in shower or grab bars that hold real weight
  • Transfer-friendly bed height

🤝 The human part

  • Staff who help without being asked twice
  • Feel-good fuel on the menu (restaurants) — meat-first options that support recovery

How the badge works today: a green ✓ FreeWheelers Approved means live Google Maps data confirms an accessible entrance plus an accessible restroom (or a feel-good-fuel menu for restaurants). Where we're headed: full 10-point on-site verification with partner venues — so the badge means someone who lives this checked it in person.
