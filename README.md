# FreeWheelers

**Access Included. Adventure Guaranteed.**

Search anywhere in the US — the coast, the mountains, a lake town, or a big city — and get matched with hotels, restaurants, and activities already vetted for your group's access needs. No phone calls, no fine print, no one left out. Just the trip.

Inspired by teammate [Art Thomas](https://lwastrong.com), founder of LWA Strong, who built a company on one idea: independence doesn't stop mattering just because a diagnosis is serious. Same idea here — pointed at something more fun than rehab.

## What it does

Type any US destination and instantly see nearby places across three categories — **Stay · Eat · Do** — each with real access details pulled live from OpenStreetMap's community-reported data.

- 🟢 **FreeWheelers Approved** — verified wheelchair-accessible AND meets our standards (accessible restroom, or feel-good-fuel menu for restaurants)
- 🟡 **Access Verified** — verified wheelchair-accessible
- 🟠 **Partially Accessible** · 🔴 **Not Accessible** · ⚪ **Not Verified — call ahead**

## Features

- Nationwide destination search with 1 / 2 / 5 / 10-mile radius, or use your current location
- Three result categories: Stay · Eat · Do
- **Feel-good fuel rules**: fast food, fried, dessert-first, and sugar-sauce BBQ are filtered out; meat-first spots (steakhouse, grill, seafood) get the badge — food that keeps everyone feeling their best
- Kid-friendly filter (real family data: kids areas, high chairs, playgrounds, zoos, parks)
- Activity search across all categories ("pool", "museum", "gym", "steak"…)
- 📸 Community Corner — post reviews with photos, no account needed
- Accessible design: large text, high contrast, keyboard-friendly

## How to run it

Download `index.html` (or `FreeWheelers-App.html` — same file) and double-click it. It opens in any browser. No install, no account, no API keys. Internet connection required for live place data.

## Data & honesty

Access details are community-reported via OpenStreetMap and may be incomplete. "Not Verified" does not mean inaccessible — it means nobody has reported it yet. Always confirm directly with the venue. FreeWheelers is an informational tool, not a guarantee of access.
